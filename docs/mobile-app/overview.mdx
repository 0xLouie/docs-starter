The Linkr mobile app is where you discover hotspots on the map and share your own.

<Info>
The app is in **invitation-only beta**. It is not on the App Store or Google Play. To
get in, [join the waitlist](https://linkrmap.com) and wait for an invitation. See
[Requesting access](/docs/getting-started/create-account).
</Info>

## What you can do

- **Discover hotspots**: browse the map of the New York City dataset
- **Read hotspot details**: speed, signal, network type, security, and contributor notes
- **Share your own hotspots**: contribute connectivity from the Share tab
- **Manage your hotspots**: view, toggle visibility, and remove hotspots you have shared

## The four tabs

The app is organized into four tabs:

- **Map**: the connectivity map, showing hotspots in the dataset
- **Share**: create and share a hotspot
- **Hotspots**: the hotspots you have shared
- **Profile**: your details and settings

Each is covered in the following pages.

## What the beta does not do yet

To set expectations for the current build:

- Hotspots you create are stored **on your device**, not on a server
- There is no cross-device sync and no web account
- Hotspots are not monitored for uptime in the background
- The app does not automatically join Wi-Fi networks for you

These are on the roadmap. See [Product status](/docs/introduction/status) for the current
picture.

The **Profile** tab holds your details and settings inside the beta app.

<Info>
The app is invitation-only. You enter it using the instructions in your invitation — see
[Requesting access](/docs/getting-started/create-account).
</Info>

## Where your data lives

In the current beta, the app keeps your data **on your device**. The hotspots you share
and your settings are stored locally.

This means:

- There is no web login, and no separate password to manage
- Your data does not sync to a server or to other devices
- Reinstalling the app or switching devices starts you fresh

<Note>
Server-side accounts, sign-in, and syncing are on the roadmap. When they land, this page
will describe how to sign in and manage your account across devices. See
[Product status](/docs/introduction/status).
</Note>

## The Profile tab

From **Profile** you can see your details and the hotspots associated with you. As the
app grows, this is where account management and preferences will live.

## Keeping your device secure

Because your Linkr data is stored on your device, standard device hygiene applies:

- Keep your device locked and up to date
- Be mindful when handing your unlocked device to someone else

The **Hotspots** tab lists everything you have shared and lets you manage it.

<Info>
The app is invitation-only beta. Your hotspots are stored on your device.
</Info>

## The Hotspots tab

Tap **Hotspots** to see the hotspots you have shared. Each entry shows its name, network
type, and visibility.

## What you can do

For each hotspot you have shared, you can:

- **View its details** — name, network type, location, and settings
- **Toggle visibility** — turn public availability on or off
- **Remove it** — delete the hotspot from your device

Removing a hotspot is permanent in the current build; there is no undo.

<Note>
Uptime graphs, reliability scores, connection counts, offline alerts, and pause/resume
are **not** in the current app. They are on the roadmap. Today the app supports sharing,
toggling visibility, and removing hotspots. See [Product status](/docs/introduction/status).
</Note>

## Keeping your list tidy

Because there is no background monitoring yet, managing your hotspots is manual:

- Remove hotspots you no longer share
- Update the name or settings by re-sharing if needed
- Keep visibility set to match how you actually want the hotspot used
